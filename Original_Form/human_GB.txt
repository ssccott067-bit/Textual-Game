Day 1

You have just woken up in one of the many laboratories stations near Calais.
You heard outside, the cries of people suffering and dying.
You came out dizzy, in total confusion. You did not remember anything.
Finally you manage to open your eyes, you contemplate the chaos: everything around you is ruined. 
The equipment is out of service, except for a dying phone and an unusable computer given its condition and age. _ 

[The first choice is offered to you, you call for a rescue team directly or wait before using the phone,] 
[knowing that it will only work for a very short time following the explosion.]

# question 1 :
Would you like to make a call (1) or wait (2) ? : 

# in case of wait :
You did not survive.
You did not know what to do because you ignored the cause of this explosion. 
By going out to inspect the damage outside the laboratory, you succumbed.

# in case of call :
Rescuers ;
"Hello, we are the emergency services. We are coming to get you, don't worry. 
Could you tell us where you are ? " _ 

You (frightened) ;
"What... what is happening ? What happened to me ? 
I heard people dying outside... I don't know if there are any survivors. " _ 

Rescuers ;
"According to our informations, you have just suffered a nuclear disaster. 
Many cities in France are affected. 
A test was carried out to link all the stations, but it failed miserably. 
The radioactivity level in the air is way too high for us to go in 
without knowing exactly where you are. Time is running out, we psssstttt... spttttttt... 
need pshhhhtttt your pstttttthhhhh addres-ppstttttttt... " _ 

The phone is cut. The rescuers ignored your precise position. _ 

You get up to analyze the surroundings and realize that the computer can be repaired 
if you manage to change a few components. _ 

# question 2
What are you going to do ? dismantle (1) / leave (2) : 

# in case of leave : 
You are dead. You did not know which components to change, 
therefore could never contact the rescuers.

# in case of dismantle : 
You now know which components to change because they are too old or damaged. 
You have created a list ; processor, microphone, PC password.

Day 2 _ 

You have your list, but you do not know where to look for it. 
Without help, finding these components seems impossible. 
Suddenly, you hear a noise in a pipe, as if something is trying to escape from it. _ 

# question 3
Are you going to, secure (1) / open (2) ? : 

# in case of secure :
You did not survive. Staying stuck in the laboratory without knowing where to look for the parts. 
You died on the spot waiting for the rescuers who never came.

# in case of open :

Day 3 _ 

A crow came out of the pipe with an envelope in its beak. 
Curious, you retrieve and open it. 
It contains a riddle. _ 
You hesitate ; could this be just a waste of time ?

# question 4
Your next step ? solve (1) / pass (2) : 

# in case of pass :
You are dead. There was certainly something important about it.

# in case of solve : 
You decide to take your time. After all, you are stuck here. 
You read the riddle out loud.

# riddle 1
[I am the department that surrounds you. Multiply my number by two,] 
[add two to it and you will have the opening code.] 
[Precision: the laboratory is near Calais.] : 

# wrong answer : 
You never found the solution. 
You lost your mind, you wonder if this crow was just a distraction. 
You died of madness.

# right answer :
# (Code 126) : (Note : 62 * 2 + 2 = 126)

The box opened ! Inside, there is a message ; 
"Bravo ! If you see this message, that means you have a great analytical capacity." _ 
There inside, you also find the processor. _ 
The crow then flies off and crashed into a box. 
You find a second crumpled letter there ; _ 

# riddle 2
[What does this word indicate ? Decode it : ZEBPQ.]  
[Hint : A = D, C = F, G = J, U = X] : 

# wrong answer :
You do not find "CHEST". You gave up and died of hunger.

# right answer :
You found the microphone in the chest.

Day 4 _ 

The crow then violently pecks a desk. 
Underneath, you find one last envelope ; _ 

# riddle 3
[Solve this calculation, the result will indicate a clockwise direction.] 
[Let a price be 16€, with a 25% reduction and an additional 2€ discount.] 
[What is the result ?] : 

# wrong answer : 
Too bad, Try again :) 

# right answer (answer = 10) : 
You look in the direction of 10 o'clock. Behind a painting, you find the password : 1234

You find yourself in front of a screen with a small piece of paper in hand. You read : 1234. _ 

A long silence settles in the ruined laboratory. 
You stare at the paper, then the computer, then the crow is looking at you with an almost mocking face.
"All this for that ? ", you mumble. _ 
"Three riddles, a department multiplication, a Caesar code and a percentage calculation... for 1234 ? 
Might as well put "password" while we're at it !" _ 

You feel a bit stupid for being tricked by death for a code that even a child could have passed by pure chance. 
But well, the balance between an effort and a reward is sometimes ironic. 
You type the keys : 1... 2... 3... 4... Enter. _ 

It works ! _ 

Without losing a second, you launch Skype and contact your loved ones. 
You ask them to call the rescuers while staying online with you to give your precise position. _ 
The signal is weak, but enough. 
A few hours later, a rescue team wearing anti-nuclear suits breaks down the armored door. _ 

As they put a protective suit on you, you look for the crow. 
But the crow had disappeared, without any doubt flew back through the pipe from which it had came. _ 

While stepping into the rescuers helicopter, tons of questions popped up in your mind ; _ 

"Does that crow really understood me or was it is just attracted by shiny objects ?"
