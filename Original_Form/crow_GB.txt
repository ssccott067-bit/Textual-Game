#

Introduction : The awakening of consciousness. _ 

Your eyes opened, you are in a crumbled place. 
Your cage was thrown to the ground by the violence of an explosion 
following a radioactive incident linked to the failure of a test 
on many nuclear power plants in France, 
the objective was to connect the nuclear plants to one another 
however this test failed and many nuclear power plants exploded, 
your cage was propelled to the ground, leaving the door open. 
You did not feel the primal instinct of a bird, but a clarity of mind that is almost frightening. _ 

This is the result of the serum injections that the scientists gave to you every single day. 
On your leg, a sealed ring indicates your identity: "Subject n°8". _ 

The air is filled with an unbearable metallic smell; 
the radioactivity saturates the atmosphere. You immediately understand that the station is serious.
Above you, a human is being. 
He is your only chance of survival. 
If he did not not manage to reset the security system, 
you will remain trapped in this laboratory. _ 


[Step 1 : The maze of pipes] _ 

Day 1 

You manage to slide into a ventilation system. 
It is the only way to the upper floors, 
but it is a real labyrinth and you did not know how to get out of this place. 
If you get lost, you will never come out of these steel walls. _ 

[Riddle 1 : The law of Labyrinth] _ 

# question 1 
How do you plan to cross this maze without getting lost ? 
'random' : You fly instantly hoping to find a way out. 
'right' : You use a geometric rule. You place your wing on the wall.
Your choice will be, random (1) / right (2) ? : 

# in case of random :
You fly in circles for hours in the dark. 
You run out of air and no longer have any strength. 
You did not survive.

# in case of right : 
The progress is slow, but the logic is foolproof. 
You finally spot a glimmer of hope. 
As you exit, you noticed that the exit door had been twisted into the shape of a 3.


[Step 2 : The terminal hydraulic] _ 

You arrived in a maintenance room located under the control post. 
To go higher, you must activate a hydraulic cylinder. 
A security screen blocks the access. _ 

[Riddle 2 : The sequence logic] _ 

# Riddle 1
The screen displays the following sequence: (50 ; 100 ; 190 ; 380 ; 750 ; 1500 ; ? ) 

To unlock the trapdoor, you must figure out the last digit. 
However, written on a maintenance note; 
"Subtract 2989 from the final result to obtain the access code".

Your answer : 

# Answer = 1 

# wrong answer :
A signal alert goes off. The security system releases a neurotoxic gas. 
You collapsed. 
You did not survive.

# right answer : 
The trapdoor opened in a hiss of steam. 
The path is clear. You manage to move forward slowly but surely, 
you got closer to the human and also mean to the freedom, 
you thank your intelligence which allowed you to solve 
this riddle but you are still mad against the scientists that caused you all this harm.


[Step 3 : The display board] _ 

Day 2 _ 

You finally joined the room where the human is. He seems helpless. 
The chest containing the necessary components for the computer is locked due to the lack of electricity. _ 
 
You found a panel with three levers. 

[Riddle 3 : The temperature test] _ 

You have three levers (1, 2 and 3). 
You can only inspect the chest once after your manipulations. _ 

You lower Lever 1 and wait 50 minutes, then you turn it off.
You lower Lever 2 and you leave it activated. 
You leave Lever 3 as it is. _ 

You go check the chest: the bulb is turned off, but the metal is too hot to touch. _ 

# Riddle 2
Which lever powered the circuit ? (1 / 2 / 3) : 

# answer 1 

# wrong answer :
It was a mistake, the bulb never turned on. 
The current is not restored and the human remains trapped. 
You did not survive.

# right answer :
That is correct. The residual heat confirms that the current had passed through. 
On the base of the lever, you saw the number 4.


[Step 4 : The secret of experiment] _ 

The human finally manages to assemble all the components on the computer, 
the computer is ready, but the human did not not know the password. 
You search through the documents scattered on the desk 
and come across your own laboratory files. _ 

[Riddle 4 : The registration number] _ 
A handwritten note of the professor indicates that ; 
"The security code is half of the half of the number of the successful experiment". 
Your ID number is 8. _ 

# Riddle 3
Answer 2 or 4 ? : 

# answer = 2 

# wrong answer :
CODE REFUSED, you perhaps should concentrate better on mathematics, 
it has caused you badluck. The computer remains locked. 
You did not survive.

# right answer :
CODE VALID. You have all the numbers: 3, 1, 4 and 2, 
you look to the right, to the left then by observing the computer, 
you notice that the password requires 4 digits. _ 

Your extraordinary intelligence tells you that the 4 digits, 
you found during your journey and your puzzle resolutions, 
are the digits of the computer password, 
but how to make the human, who is less intelligent than you, understand it. _ 


[Conclusion : Farewell to servitude] _ 

You fly over a desk and you land on a small mirror, 
that somehow survived the shock. _ 
The room is freezing, but your breath, panting hardly, is warm. 
You bring your beak close to the cold surface of the glass. 
By strongly exhaling, you created a layer of mist on the mirror. _ 
With a precise tap of your claw, you trace a message ; 
"an envelope is taped under this desk" 
you attracted the human's attention by pecking the desk so that he come towards you, 
and reads your message. _ 

The human approached and stunned, he reads your message, 
then he grabs the envelope taped under the table, opened it and read it loudly. _ 
The message says "Arrange the digits in ascending order (1-2-3-4). 
This is the computer password". He enters the code. 
The system activated, you saw the rescue teams. _ 

The rescuers, dressed in the anti-radiation suits, 
come to rescue the human and leave by using helicopter. _ 

From the distance, you hear the dull sound of the helicopters. 
The human turn towards you, with a look filled with gratitude. 
He seems to want to protect you, to take you with him. _ 
But you know what the protection of human means: new cages, new tests. 
You sat on the ledge of a broken window. 
You look at the human one last time. You let out a powerful cry, 
a wild note that seems like a goodbye to your old life as a lab rat. _ 
Without looking back, you spread your wings and fly towards the horizon of Calais. 

You are finally free.
