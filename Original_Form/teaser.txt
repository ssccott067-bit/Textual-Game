[Teaser of Mysterious Crow]

In an apocalyptic world, you embody either a crow or a human who had just awakened in a ruined laboratory, 
surrounded by a desolate and radioactive wasteland. 
In your quest, the objective is twofold. 

The human must search for computer components to upgrade the only functioning yet ancient computer left in the lab. 
The city of Calais is currently inaccessible due to the extreme levels of radiation in the air; 
however, you do not have any equipment to escape, nor any tools powerful enough to reach for any help. 
You will face numerous puzzles to recover some of the computer components
in order to establish a contact with the outside world and be evacuated. 

The crow, an extraordinary intelligence being, 
have another task to do that will also help the human escape. 
While, the human’s role is to gather the components needed, you will help the human with a very primordial problem. 

Only your intelligence can save you. 
Depending on the character chosen at the beginning, you will have different task as well path.
Each of these two characters possessed their own distinct traits and assets.

Do you have what it takes to survive and escape ?
